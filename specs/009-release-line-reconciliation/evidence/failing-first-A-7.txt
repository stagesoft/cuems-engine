## Why it is red, and what differs from the task text (T019)

T019 expected the red to be "resolves to system:capture_1" (the symptom 35cbe78 describes, produced by
iterating a *list* of port-type dicts). It is red for an earlier reason: on the 0.1.1 library,
`cm.node_mappings.get("audio")` is a single typed `DeviceType` (`{"outputs": [[{"output": {...}}, …]],
"inputs": [[{"input": {...}}, …]], "class": "audio"}`), so `for port_type_dict in …get("audio", [])` iterates its
*keys* and `port_type_dict.values()` raises `AttributeError: 'str' object has no attribute 'values'`.
`set_audio_players`/`set_video_players` therefore cannot run at all against the typed mappings (008's
migration did not reach them; no existing test calls them with real mappings).

Consequence for T020: merging 35cbe78 by SHA is not enough. Its hunks assume the old iteration and call
`port_type_dict.get("outputs", [])` on what is a str here. The A-7 adaptation (typed read, outputs only) goes
in right after the merge, red-first against this same test.
